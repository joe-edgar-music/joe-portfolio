## About The Effect
The Effect is a play by Lucy Prebble, about two young people who participate in an antidepressant drug trial, only to find themselves falling in love, but unsure if it’s real or just the effects of the drug. I was lucky enough to provide the music and sound design for a production of the play by Sheffield University Theatre Company (SUTCo), directed by Emilia Lodge.  

The company was delightful to work with and I learned a huge amount about producing sound and music for theatre on this show. I’m so glad I got to contribute to such an epic production.  

## Continue for musical stuff
The music for the show was heavily electronic, and primarily based on a beeping ECG sound effect, which I created in a synth so I could vary it more easily over the course of the show. However, I was still able to include performances from a wonderful string trio, consisting of Charlotte Joos, Emily Spitzer, and Iris Rea, and some flute from the ever-incredible Sam Jordan.
