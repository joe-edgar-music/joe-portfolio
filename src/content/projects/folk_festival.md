On 3rd May 2025, the newly-formed Sheffield University Folk Music Society held its first ever festival at Crookes Social Club. We showcased music arranged and performed by the society and bands made up of its members, as well as a performance from the fantastic newcastle-based trio Rillen. The day concluded with a ceilidh from our counterparts in the ceilidh society.  

This event was the culmination of four months of hard work from myself and Theo, as well as the amazing society members who helped. From the idea’s conception in January, we first had to actually become a society, and then navigate all of the challenges that organising this event threw at us: finding a venue, securing funding (we started with £50), communicating with performers and completing administrative tasks from the Student’s Union. All of this, we did whilst also running the society on a day-to-day, arranging and learning our set to perform at the festival!  

On the day, we faced technical challenges, and the constant fear of how well the event would go. In the end however, it was a massive success: all the performances sounded great, we had well over 100 people in attendance, and made over £800 in ticket sales.  

Special thanks must be given to a few people: Kate Griffin, for her fantastic photos (above); Kit & Nat for being our sound technicians; and John, Rowen, & Lily from the Ceilidh Society for organising and running the ceilidh for us.  

I had never even attempted something like this before. From it, I learned to juggle all aspects of managing an event, both behind the scenes and on the day.
