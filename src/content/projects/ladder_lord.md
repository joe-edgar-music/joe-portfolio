## About Ladder Lord
Ladder Lord is a physics puzzle game created for the Game Maker's Toolkit Game Jam 2024. The player controls a soldier beseiging a castle, and tries to throw a ladder to scale the wall.

## The Music
I created the music to fit the game's 8-bit style, whilst maintaing the medieval theme. It uses FMOD for interactive music, including a general play theme which transitions to a "near the end of the level" theme, as well as game over and victory stings.
