## About "Live From Jessop"
In my final year of university, I led Sheffield Folk Society on our biggest project yet: *Live From Jessop*, an album featuring arrangements of traditional and contemporary folk tunes and songs, plus additional material from the society's members. The project was a huge success, and I am enormously proud of Folk Society for creating something so fantastic.

## The Music
The music on the album is a mix of traditional and contemporary folk music, originating in Ireland, England, Scotland, America, and more. The performances of the society's main band were arranged primarily by myself and co-founder Theo Hunter, with input from the rest of the society.  

Calyx are Theo Hunter and Pete Cowley. That's The Badger are Amelia Wallis, Tamsin Rake, and Sam Jordan.

## Creating the Album
As Folk Society's secretary, I did a huge amount of co-ordination and project management to get the album made. I collaborated with Octagon Records, the University's student-run record label, and with local venues like Sidney & Matilda for promotional gigs. Alongside the amazing Tom Dallimore & Finlay Sandham, I helped to record the album, which was primarily done at community run venue Spring Tank Studios (the title "Live From Jessop" may not be strictly true...).  

The album was great fun to work on, and everybody involved put in a huge amount of hard work, so please give it a listen! It is available to stream everywhere. My personal highlights are The Cuckoo, Westlin' Winds, and Catharsis.
